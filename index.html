Attack Sprites

Notes

  • DM20, Pen20, DMX and PenZ have a Strong and Weak attack.
  • DMC supports an Alt attack but there is no difference in power.
  • Pen20 and DMC attack IDs are the same for 0-116.
  • Attack 117 only exists on Pen20 and is unused; it was assigned a DMC number here for convenience.
  • Attack 118 only exists on DMX and was assigned a Pen20/DMC number here for convenience.
